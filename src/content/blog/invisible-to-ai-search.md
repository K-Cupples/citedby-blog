I've been digging into the data on AI search for the last few months. Not the hype, not the hot takes. The actual numbers on which brands show up when people ask AI for recommendations, and which ones don't.

The short version: most brands are completely invisible.

A study of 2,089 brands found that 77% scored below 5 out of 100 on AI visibility. Not small brands. Brands with real traffic, real customers, real Google rankings. They just don't exist in AI search.

And this isn't a small problem that can wait. ChatGPT has 900 million weekly active users. Perplexity grew 100% in 2025. AI referral traffic is up 357% year over year. When someone asks "what's the best tool for X" and your brand isn't in the answer, that's a customer you'll never know you lost.

## Each AI Platform Is Its Own Ecosystem

This is the part that surprised me most. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews don't pull from the same places. At all.

A study of 680 million citations across all three platforms found:

- **ChatGPT** leans on Wikipedia. 47.9% of its top citations come from there. It favors encyclopedic, consensus-based content.
- **Perplexity** leans on Reddit. 46.7% of its top citations are Reddit threads. It also cites 2.76x more sources per answer than ChatGPT (21.87 vs 7.92).
- **Google AI Overviews** spread across YouTube (18.8%), LinkedIn, and Quora. More multi-modal, less concentrated.

Only 11% of domains get cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity. Being visible on one platform doesn't mean you're visible on another. They're essentially different search engines with different source preferences.

And here's one that should change how you think about SEO: only 12% of URLs cited by AI tools match Google's top 10 results for the same query. Your Google rankings and your AI visibility are mostly separate.

## The Zero-Click Problem Is Getting Worse

58.5% of US Google searches already end without anyone clicking a link. The user gets their answer right on the page.

When Google's AI Overviews show up, it gets more extreme. Organic click-through rates drop 61%. Paid click-through rates drop 68%. The AI answers the question and the user moves on.

But there's a flip side. Brands that get cited inside AI Overviews earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks compared to brands that appear in results but aren't cited in the AI answer. Being mentioned by AI is becoming a competitive advantage, not just in AI search, but in Google too.

## Reddit Is the Most Cited Source Across AI Platforms

If there's one thing I didn't expect, it's how much Reddit matters.

Across all AI platforms combined, Reddit is the single most-cited domain at 40.1% of total citations. More than Wikipedia. More than YouTube. More than any news outlet.

For Perplexity specifically, Reddit accounts for nearly half of all citations. That means the Reddit threads that rank on Google for your keywords are the same threads AI is reading and citing back to users. If your brand is mentioned in those threads, AI knows about you. If it's not, AI doesn't.

## What Actually Gets You Cited

Research from Princeton and Georgia Tech (published at KDD 2024) studied what makes AI engines cite certain content over others. The findings:

- Adding statistics to your content boosts AI visibility by 30-40%
- Citing credible sources and including quotes from experts helps significantly
- Content structure matters. Well-organized, extractable content gets cited more than walls of text

From the broader data, the pattern is clear. AI engines favor:

1. **Third-party mentions over your own website.** AI trusts what others say about you more than what you say about yourself. Reddit threads, review sites, and earned media carry more weight than your homepage.
2. **Original data and research.** 67% of ChatGPT's top citations go to first-hand research and proprietary data. If you have unique data, publish it.
3. **Consistent entity information.** Brands with the same name, description, and positioning across every platform are easier for AI to recognize and reference.
4. **Fresh content.** AI models favor recently published and recently updated content. Static pages from 2021 don't perform well.

## The Opportunity Is Right Now

Here's the stat that matters most to me: only 11% of B2B companies have 75% or more of their content ready for AI discovery. 35% of businesses don't even know that AI search optimization is a thing.

AI search currently drives about 1% of total web traffic. That sounds small until you realize it was basically 0% two years ago and it's growing 357% year over year. E-commerce AI traffic grew 4,700% in the last year alone.

The brands that figure this out early will have a massive advantage. The ones that wait until AI search is 10-20% of traffic will be playing catch-up against competitors who've been building visibility for years.

54% of marketers say they plan to implement AI search optimization within 3-6 months. The window to be early is closing.

AI search isn't replacing Google. It's adding a whole new layer on top of it. And right now, most brands aren't even playing the game.
